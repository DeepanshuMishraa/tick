## Design Context

### Users

People on Apple Silicon Macs who want to turn spoken thoughts into text while working in any app. The primary job is simple: press a shortcut, speak, and get clean text at the cursor without sending audio to a cloud service.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, considered. The product should feel like a small native Mac utility that respects the user's attention rather than a loud productivity platform.

### Aesthetic Direction

Super-minimal editorial landing page. Warm off-white canvas, near-black typography, one restrained lilac accent borrowed from the product artwork, generous whitespace, crisp geometry, and subtle motion. No dashboard-like sections, bento grids, heavy shadows, gradients, or novelty UI chrome.

### Design Principles

- Let the product promise and typography do most of the work.
- Keep the interface calm, direct, and unmistakably native to macOS.
- Use one visual anchor with enough scale to create confidence.
- Make the download action obvious without making the page feel salesy.
- Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion support.
