Hold a key. Speak. It's typed.

Tick is a private voice-to-text utility for macOS. Transcription runs on your Mac, then the text is pasted into whatever app you're typing in. No account, no subscription.

$ brew install --cask deepanshumishraa/tap/tick

download for macOS apple silicon · macOS 15+

How it works

01holdPress and hold your hotkey, speak, release. Tick transcribes the moment you let go.
02lockDouble-tap the hotkey for hands-free dictation. Tap once more to finish.
03pasteText is cleaned up and pasted at your cursor, in any app.

privateTranscription runs on your Mac. Audio is never uploaded.
formattingOptional AI cleanup with an on-device Qwen3 model, or Groq if you add an API key.
dictionaryTeach it names and jargon. It also learns from your corrections.
historyPast transcripts, stored on your Mac.
